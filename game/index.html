SOLMAR BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

Rico Vega came back to Solmar Bay with empty pockets and a fast reputation. Fixer Marisol Reyes has five jobs that pay real money, the SBPD has five stars of patience, and every car on Palm Ave is a ride waiting to be borrowed.

Explore the city on foot or by car, steal what you need, run jobs, lose the cops, collect Bay coins, and take taxi fares on the side. The bay is yours if you can hold it.

W A S D — walk / drive
E — steal car / exit / interact
Space — handbrake
Shift — sprint
F — punch
M — mute   Esc — pause

Solmar Bay is an original game. Its city, characters, vehicles, names and story are invented for this game. No network requests: everything runs from these files.
